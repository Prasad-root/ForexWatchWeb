Privacy policy

Starter text only. Have it reviewed and adapted to your real data practices before launch. Google also requires a privacy policy URL to publish your sign-in app.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google.
  • Usage data: basic logs such as pages visited and datasets downloaded.
  • Messages: anything you send through the contact form.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your name and email address with us. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Google files, contacts or other data.

How we use it

To run your account, let you download datasets, reply to your messages and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your account at any time through the contact page.
