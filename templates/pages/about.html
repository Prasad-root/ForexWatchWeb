Our Mission & What We Offer

Good decisions start with reliable, accessible data. We organize Sri Lanka's core market indicators into a single dashboard so households, businesses, students, and researchers can work from the same facts.

Commercial Bank Exchange Rates

Daily TT buying and selling rates across major Sri Lankan banks, with the most competitive market rates automatically highlighted.

Essential Goods & Fuel Prices

Up-to-date retail market prices for daily necessities alongside official island-wide fuel price revisions at a glance.

Stock Market Tracking

Key Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) indices including ASPI and S&P SL20, paired with leading share movements.

Open Data & Analytics

Historical trend visualizations and free, structured datasets engineered for academic research and market analysis.
