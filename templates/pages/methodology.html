Methodology & data sources

How the numbers on {{ SITE_NAME }} are collected, checked and published.

Replace the placeholders below with your real sources and update times before launch. Credibility pages like this one matter a lot for a data site.

Exchange rates

Source: published buy and sell rate sheets of licensed commercial banks. Update time: once each business day. Definition: rates are LKR per one unit of foreign currency (per 100 for JPY).

Commodity prices

Source: daily retail market price reports. Definition: average retail price in LKR per the stated unit.

Fuel prices

Source: official price announcements from Ceylon Petroleum Corporation and Lanka IOC. Prices change on revision dates and stay the same in between.

Stock market

Source: Colombo Stock Exchange end-of-day data. Index values and share prices are closing values.

Quality checks

  • Values outside an expected range are flagged for review before publishing.
  • Missing days are left blank, never estimated.
  • Corrections are published with a note. Report an error through the contact page.

Disclaimer

Information is provided for general and educational purposes only. It is not financial advice. Check rates with your bank before transacting.
